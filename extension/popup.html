      What you share

      During active monitoring, Vouch records tab switches and changes to the website in your active tab in the focused Chrome window. Each event includes the time and a browser tab ID. For website changes, Vouch also records the hostname, such as example.com. Your employer can review these events.

      Vouch does not collect keystrokes, screenshots, clipboard or page contents, page titles, or full URLs with paths, queries or fragments. It cannot see activity in other applications, devices or browsers.

      Joining and granting consent leave monitoring off until you choose Start Monitoring. You can decline consent, withdraw it later, or stop monitoring at any time. Once stopped, monitoring cannot restart for this interview.
